Article 1. Scope of the Service

  6. Multi-user editing and cloud synchronization.
  7. Private, link-only, and public trip settings.
  8. Likes ("hearts"), ranking, and authorized copying of public trips.
  9. Reading mode, data export, and version management.
  10. Uploading trip photos, custom notes, and stamps.
  11. From the trip's departure date, recording "visited / skipped / unmarked (unknown)" and optionally the actual amount spent.
